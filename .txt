Design and develop a premium, modern, elegant Bengali news portal named **খবরনামা (Khobornama)**.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Brand Name: খবরনামা
- English Name: Khobornama
- Industry: Bengali News & Media
- Personality: Premium, Minimal, Trustworthy, Editorial, Elegant.

OFFICIAL BRAND COLORS:
- Primary: #202020
- Background: #E4E1DC
- Secondary Background: #F8F7F4
- Accent: #34483C

LOGO:
Use the official Khobornama logo:

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Bengali: Hind Siliguri
- English: Inter

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Create a professional newspaper-inspired interface with clean layouts, excellent typography, balanced whitespace, subtle borders, elegant news cards, and strong visual hierarchy.

Use:
- Dark charcoal for text and footer.
- Warm off-white for content surfaces.
- Soft warm grey for page backgrounds.
- Deep Islamic green for accents, buttons, active navigation, and highlights.

WEBSITE CATEGORIES:
জাতীয়, রাজনীতি, অর্থনীতি, আন্তর্জাতিক, সারাদেশ, খেলা, ধর্ম, স্বাস্থ্য, ভিডিও, কলাম, ফিচার, মতামত, সাহিত্য, আইন ও আদালত, অন্যান্য।

MAIN FEATURES:
- Responsive navigation and mobile menu.
- Breaking news ticker.
- Latest and popular news sections.
- Category-based news browsing.
- Featured news section.
- News search.
- Article details with related news.
- Social sharing.
- Advertisement placements.
- Professional footer.
- Complete admin dashboard and content management.

IMPORTANT:
Maintain consistent branding throughout every page. Do not introduce unrelated colors, excessive gradients, unnecessary animations, or cluttered layouts. Prioritize readability, performance, accessibility, responsiveness, and a premium editorial appearance.
